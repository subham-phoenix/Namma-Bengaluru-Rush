Namma Bengaluru RushA tiny open world in eight missions

ನಮ್ಮ ಬೆಂಗಳೂರು

Hop into an auto, race the Purple Line, chase a thief through KR Market, play gully cricket in Cubbon Park, run a pub quiz on Church Street, make the airport run and deliver a hot masala dosa across town. Traffic obeys the signals, pedestrians have opinions, and a cow may be standing in the road.

This is a stylised, compressed Bengaluru anchored to real landmark positions, so real distances are much longer (the airport is about 35 km out in real life). The real Purple Line runs underground between Cubbon Park and the City Railway Station, including Majestic. Here the viaduct is elevated end to end to keep the race readable.

Heads up: this game needs a keyboard and mouse. It will not play well on a phone or tablet.
